## Eight creative topics

1. Vibe: global, premium, immersive, community, innovation (provided brief). Authored references: festival programme typography, documentary contact sheets, an event wristband/passport.
2. Journey: event introduction, next edition, tracks, past speakers, event history, ecosystem/partners, involvement and registration, contact/social footer (requirements supplied; ordering authored).
3. Energy: immediate confident opening, compact logistical pause, immersive track discovery peak, human faces, photographic memories, quiet partner proof, decisive orange ending.
4. Feeling: curiosity → clarity → agency → trust → belonging → confidence → resolve. Peak: “I chose my interests and watched my own Carnival Passport come together.”
5. Signature: choosing tracks visibly stamps a personal Carnival Passport, which carries into a three-step registration preview and a downloadable personal plan. No wallet, payment or claim of minted NFTs.
6. Aesthetic: expressive festival editorial. Ink canvas, off-white type, original white logo, proposed vermilion accent; sharp rules, generous space, mixed photo sizes. Two sans-serif families maximum.
7. Distinct scenes chosen for usable event navigation. No continuous flight; no scroll hijacking. Full information available without motion.
8. Assets: official website supplies logo, speaker portraits, event artwork and contact links. Real documentary event photographs sought from official public pages. Any illustrative stock photograph is explicitly labelled, never presented as a particular event or person.

## Grammar: Festival programme

New structural grammar justified by a navigable programme, not a linear pitch: persistent section index and passport count, opening as a layered festival masthead, discrete grounds with compact logistics, interactive track selection as the largest desktop spread, portrait directory, dated archive, and a perforated-passport closing invitation. Forbids video scrubbing, full-page pinning, forced horizontal scrolling, inaccessible hover-only content, repeated section templates and invented scarcity.

Other grammars: filmic hides needed navigation; editorial title-page rule loses immediate photography; live surface forbids required brand storytelling; continuous world has no geographic journey; poster forbids required photography/cards; catalog forbids introductory event context; split stage has no honest two-sided comparison; cutlist's pacing is too fast for track discovery.

## Hero layer contract

Far: ink ground and oversized outlined event lettering, smallest motion. Mid: documentary photo mosaic, restrained vertical parallax. Near: orange ticket-shaped link and small photographic frame, slightly faster motion. Typography is stable semantic HTML above the composition with clear solid ground. No extracted people, therefore no duplicate-subject or contact-point risks. Opening shows headline/photo overlap; midpoint separates photos and near ticket; exit naturally reveals logistics. Mobile stacks headline, central photo and CTA with reduced displacement. Reduced motion is complete and static.
